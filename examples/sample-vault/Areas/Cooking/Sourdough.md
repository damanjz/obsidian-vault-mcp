# Sourdough

Keep the starter at room temperature.

## Feeding schedule

Feed once a day with equal weights of flour and water. A healthy starter
doubles in four to six hours.

## Discard

Use discard for crackers or pancakes. Extra discard goes to the compost for the
[[Garden Planner|garden]].
